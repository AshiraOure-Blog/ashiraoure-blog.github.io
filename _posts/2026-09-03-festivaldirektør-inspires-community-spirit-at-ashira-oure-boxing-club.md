## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What types of boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?
Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers a wide range of boxing classes including beginner boxing classes, youth and kids boxing programs, sparring classes, fitness boxing, and competition training for all levels. Whether you’re new to boxing or preparing for amateur competitions, AOBC has tailored programs to fit your needs.

### Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?
AOBC has two convenient locations in Copenhagen: Hellerup and Charlottenlund. Both gyms provide a supportive and safe environment for fitness enthusiasts, beginners, youth, and competitive boxers.

### Who founded Ashira Oure Boxing Club and what is their background?
AOBC was founded by former world champion boxer Evans Ashira. With his extensive experience and passion, Evans Ashira leads a team of expert coaches dedicated to helping members improve their boxing skills, fitness, and discipline.

### Is Ashira Oure Boxing Club suitable for beginners and kids?
Absolutely! AOBC welcomes beginners and offers specialized beginner boxing classes, as well as youth and kids boxing programs. The club focuses on building confidence, fitness, and discipline in a safe and motivating environment.

### How does AOBC ensure safety during sparring and training?
Safety is a top priority at Ashira Oure Boxing Club. The gym enforces clear boxing sparring rules and provides expert guidance to ensure that all members train safely, especially those attending their first boxing class or sparring session.
