## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What types of boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer for beginners in Copenhagen?  
Ashira Oure Boxing Club provides beginner boxing classes designed to introduce newcomers to the fundamentals of boxing, emphasizing safety, proper technique, and fitness. These classes are ideal for those looking for a supportive environment to start their boxing journey in Copenhagen.

### Where are the Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?  
AOBC has two convenient locations in Copenhagen: Hellerup and Charlottenlund. Both gyms offer state-of-the-art facilities and expert coaching catering to all skill levels, from beginner boxing to competition training.

### Does AOBC offer youth and kids boxing programs near me in Copenhagen?  
Yes, Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers specialized youth boxing programs and kids boxing classes that focus on building athletic skills, confidence, and discipline in a safe and encouraging environment.

### How does Ashira Oure Boxing Club prepare boxers for competition?  
AOBC provides advanced sparring classes and personalized competition training plans. Under the guidance of world champion Evans Ashira and experienced coaches, members receive comprehensive support to excel in amateur and competitive boxing.

### Can I combine boxing and fitness training at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?  
Absolutely. AOBC offers boxing and fitness classes that blend skill development with cardiovascular and strength training, making it an excellent gym with boxing options for fitness enthusiasts in Copenhagen.
