## Building Resilience—Inside and Out

Just as Copenhagen’s weather builds character, boxing at AOBC builds resilience. Our members, from complete beginners to seasoned boxers, develop confidence, discipline, and camaraderie—qualities that last long after you leave the gym.

- Connect with fellow members—our community is your support system, rain or shine.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What types of boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer for beginners in Copenhagen?

Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers beginner boxing classes tailored to those new to the sport. These classes focus on fundamental skills, fitness, and building confidence in a supportive environment perfect for newcomers to boxing in Copenhagen.

### Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?

AOBC has two convenient locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund. Both gyms provide top-tier facilities and coaching for boxers of all ages and skill levels.

### Can kids join boxing classes at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?

Yes, AOBC offers specialized youth and kids boxing programs designed to promote fitness, discipline, and fun. These classes are great for children looking to start boxing or improve their skills in a safe environment.

### How does Ashira Oure Boxing Club support competitive boxing training?

AOBC provides sparring classes and boxing competition training led by experienced coaches, including former world champion Evans Ashira. These programs prepare amateur and competitive boxers for tournaments and fights with advanced techniques and conditioning.

### What makes Ashira Oure Boxing Club stand out among boxing gyms in Copenhagen?

AOBC stands out due to its expert coaching team, led by Evans Ashira, comprehensive programs for all levels, community focus, and commitment to fitness and discipline. The club’s supportive atmosphere and top facilities make it a top choice for boxing enthusiasts in Copenhagen.
