Founded by former world champion **Evans Ashira**, AOBC stands out as Copenhagen’s premier boxing gym, welcoming everyone from absolute beginners to seasoned champions.

## Motivation Starts with Community at Ashira Oure Boxing Club in Copenhagen

When you join AOBC, you’re not just signing up for boxing classes; you’re becoming part of a supportive community. Members—whether they train at our **Hellerup boxing gym** or **Charlottenlund location**—find encouragement and camaraderie in every session. Our trainers and members inspire one another, building motivation that lasts far beyond the ring.

### Why Motivation Matters at AOBC – Your Boxing Club Copenhagen

- **For Beginners:** Starting something new can feel overwhelming. Our **beginner boxing classes** and **boxing club for beginners** are designed to help you take those first steps confidently, surrounded by others who are on the same journey.
- **For Fitness Enthusiasts:** If you want more than a typical gym routine, AOBC’s **boxing and fitness classes** blend high-energy workouts with skill-building, keeping you motivated as you push toward your goals.
- **For Youth and Kids:** AOBC is a trusted place for **youth boxing** and **boxing training for kids**. Our coaches foster motivation through fun, structured classes that teach discipline and self-confidence.
- **For Competitive Boxers:** Ambition drives our **boxing competition training** and **sparring classes**. With guidance from Evans Ashira and our expert team, you’ll find the motivation needed to elevate your performance.

## Expert Coaching Fuels Inspiration at Ashira Oure Boxing Club

Motivation grows with mentorship. At AOBC, every class is led by experienced coaches—including **Evans Ashira** himself—who know how to ignite passion and perseverance in every member. Our team, including **Yuna Ashira** and **Lucas Ashira**, helps you set and reach new milestones, whether you’re preparing for your first sparring session or your next tournament.

## Tailored Training for Every Goal at AOBC Boxing Gym Copenhagen

No matter your experience level, AOBC offers programs to match your motivation:

- **Personal Coaching:** Customized plans built around your goals, schedule, and fitness level.
- **Sparring and Drills:** Develop skills and confidence in a safe, respectful environment where motivation is fostered by friendly competition.
- **Fitness Boxing:** Dynamic workouts that combine boxing technique with high-intensity fitness.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?

Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers beginner boxing classes tailored for all levels in Copenhagen, including Hellerup and Charlottenlund. These classes focus on foundational skills, fitness, and building confidence in a supportive environment.

### Is AOBC suitable for youth and kids interested in boxing?

Yes, AOBC provides specialized youth boxing and boxing training for kids, emphasizing discipline, motivation, and fun to encourage healthy development and confidence.

### Who founded Ashira Oure Boxing Club, and what is their background?

AOBC was founded by former world champion **Evans Ashira**, who brings expert knowledge and experience to coaching all levels, from beginners to competitive boxers.

### Are there sparring and competition training classes available at AOBC?

Absolutely. AOBC offers dedicated sparring classes and boxing competition training designed to prepare members for amateur boxing competitions safely and effectively.

### Where are the Ashira Oure Boxing Club gyms located in Copenhagen?

AOBC has two convenient locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund, serving the greater Copenhagen boxing community.
