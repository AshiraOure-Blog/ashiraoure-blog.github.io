- **Youth and Kids’ Programs:** Our youth boxing and kids boxing near me sessions are designed to build confidence, discipline, and athletic ability in a safe, nurturing environment.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What beginner boxing classes are available at Ashira Oure Boxing Club in Copenhagen?
Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers beginner boxing classes tailored for all fitness levels. These classes focus on foundational boxing drills, fitness boxing techniques, and safety tips to ensure newcomers feel confident and supported.

### Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?
AOBC has two prime locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund. Both gyms provide state-of-the-art facilities for boxing training, fitness workouts, sparring sessions, and youth programs.

### Does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer training for youth and kids?
Yes, AOBC runs dedicated youth boxing and kids boxing classes designed to build discipline, athletic skills, and confidence in a safe and nurturing environment, perfect for parents searching for "kids boxing near me" in Copenhagen.

### How can I prepare for boxing competition training at AOBC?
At Ashira Oure Boxing Club, competition prep includes personalized coaching, advanced sparring classes, and a structured boxing training schedule that focuses on skill, fitness, and mental readiness under the guidance of former world champion Evans Ashira.

### Is Ashira Oure Boxing Club suitable for fitness enthusiasts?
Absolutely. AOBC combines boxing and fitness classes to create effective boxing fitness workouts that improve overall health, strength, and endurance, making it an ideal gym with boxing for fitness enthusiasts in Copenhagen.
