## Why Protein Matters for Boxers in Copenhagen

Every jab, hook, and uppercut relies on strong, well-recovered muscles. Protein is essential for repairing and rebuilding muscle tissue after intense training sessions—something our members experience daily, whether in our Hellerup or Charlottenlund boxing gyms. When your body gets the protein it needs, you recover faster, grow stronger, and stay ready for your next session on the bags or in the ring.

### Key Benefits of Protein for Boxing and Fitness Boxing

- **Muscle Repair**: After a tough sparring class or boxing competition training, protein helps your muscles rebuild, reducing soreness and risk of injury.
- **Endurance and Power**: Consistent protein intake supports lean muscle, giving you the explosive strength needed in every round, from beginner boxing classes to advanced amateur boxing club sessions.
- **Satiety and Weight Management**: For those pursuing fitness boxing or looking to maintain a healthy weight, protein-rich meals keep you feeling full and energized, supporting your goals inside and outside the gym.

## How Much Protein Do You Need for Boxing Training?

Your individual needs depend on age, training intensity, and goals. As a guideline, most active adults benefit from 1.2 to 2.0 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day. Youth boxing participants and those in competition prep may need a bit more to support growth and performance.

At AOBC, our coaches—including Evans Ashira himself—are always ready to help you tailor your boxing workout plan and discuss how nutrition, like protein intake, fits your boxing training schedule in Copenhagen.

## The Best Sources of Protein for Boxers in Denmark

Being part of the Ashira Oure Boxing Club means striving for excellence in every area—including what you put on your plate. Here are some top protein picks for boxers training in Copenhagen and nearby regions:

- **Lean Meats** (chicken, turkey, lean beef)
- **Fish and Seafood**
- **Eggs**
- **Dairy Products** (yogurt, cottage cheese, milk)
- **Plant-Based Options** (beans, lentils, tofu, tempeh, quinoa)

Local markets in Copenhagen offer fresh, high-quality ingredients to support your boxing and fitness journey. Whether you’re fueling up before a youth boxing class or recovering post-workout with a protein-packed snack, making mindful choices pays off in the ring.

## Timing Your Protein Intake for Optimal Boxing Performance

It’s not just what you eat—it’s when you eat. For maximum results, aim to consume protein within 30-60 minutes after your training session. This helps speed up recovery and muscle growth, so you’re always ready for your next AOBC session—be it sparring classes, fitness boxing, or a boxing class for beginners.
