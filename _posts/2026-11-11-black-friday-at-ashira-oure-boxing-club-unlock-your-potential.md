## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?  
AOBC provides beginner boxing classes tailored for those new to the sport. These classes focus on fundamental techniques, fitness boxing, and building confidence in a supportive environment at both Hellerup and Charlottenlund locations.

### How can I join youth or kids boxing programs at AOBC?  
Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers specialized youth boxing programs and boxing training for kids designed to promote fitness, discipline, and fun. Parents can contact AOBC directly to learn about schedules and enrollment for youth boxing classes near Copenhagen.

### What makes Ashira Oure Boxing Club different from other boxing gyms in Copenhagen?  
AOBC stands out due to its experienced coaching staff led by former world champion Evans Ashira, a comprehensive approach combining boxing skill, fitness, and wellness, and a welcoming community atmosphere for all levels, from beginners to competitive boxers.

### Are sparring and boxing competition training available at AOBC?  
Yes, Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers sparring classes and dedicated boxing competition training to prepare amateur and aspiring professional boxers for bouts, emphasizing safety and proper boxing sparring rules.

### Can I get personal coaching and customized boxing workout plans at AOBC?  
AOBC provides personal coaching tailored to individual goals, including fitness boxing, boxing drills, and boxing training schedules to help members progress effectively whether training for fitness or competition.
