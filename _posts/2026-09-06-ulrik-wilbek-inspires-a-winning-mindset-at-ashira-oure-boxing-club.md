At Ashira Oure Boxing Club, Copenhagen’s premier boxing gym, determination, skill, and community come together to create champions—both in and out of the ring. Inspired by the leadership and motivational spirit of figures like Ulrik Wilbek, AOBC stands as a beacon for anyone ready to embrace the challenge of boxing, whether you’re stepping onto the mat for your first class or preparing for your next competition.

## Building Champions in Copenhagen

Founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, Ashira Oure Boxing Club (AOBC) offers much more than a typical boxing gym experience. With state-of-the-art facilities in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, AOBC is renowned as the boxing club Copenhagen trusts for expert instruction, supportive community, and a relentless focus on personal growth.

### What Sets AOBC Apart?

- **Expert-Led Classes**: Every session, from beginner boxing classes to advanced sparring, is guided by experienced coaches, including Evans Ashira himself.
- **Inclusive Community**: AOBC welcomes everyone—absolute beginners, seasoned athletes, youth, and families. You’ll find a supportive environment that encourages you to push your limits.
- **Comprehensive Programs**: The club offers youth boxing, boxing training for kids, fitness boxing, competition prep, and personal coaching. Whether you’re looking for fitness or to pursue amateur boxing, AOBC has a program for you.
- **Discipline & Wellness**: Inspired by leaders like Ulrik Wilbek, AOBC emphasizes discipline, resilience, and holistic wellness—values that help boxers thrive in and out of the ring.

## Why Boxing? Why AOBC?

Boxing is more than a sport. It’s a powerful way to build confidence, strength, and mental focus. At Ashira Oure Boxing Club, you’ll gain:

- **Physical Fitness**: Dynamic boxing workouts improve endurance, strength, and agility.
- **Life Skills**: Boxing teaches discipline, goal-setting, and perseverance, qualities championed by leaders such as Ulrik Wilbek.
- **A Supportive Network**: AOBC’s boxing community is known for its welcoming spirit, inspiring members to achieve their best.

### Programs for Every Level

Whether you’re searching for “beginner boxing classes,” “boxing club for beginners,” or “youth boxing near me,” AOBC provides tailored programs:

- **Kids & Teens**: Fun, safe, and structured youth boxing classes designed to build confidence and fitness.
- **Adults**: From fitness-focused sessions to sparring classes and boxing competition training, there’s a place for everyone.
- **Personal Coaching**: Work one-on-one with experienced trainers like Evans Ashira to sharpen your skills and achieve your personal goals.

## Join the AOBC Family in Copenhagen

Ashira Oure Boxing Club isn’t just a gym—it’s a community where every member is empowered to grow stronger, healthier, and more confident. Whether you’re in Hellerup, Charlottenlund, or anywhere in Copenhagen, AOBC is your local destination for world-class boxing and fitness.
