## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?  
AOBC offers beginner boxing classes designed for all ages and fitness levels. These classes focus on fundamental boxing techniques, fitness, and building confidence in a supportive environment.

### Where are the Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?  
AOBC has two main locations in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, both conveniently accessible for residents of Tingbjerg and greater Copenhagen.

### Does AOBC provide youth boxing programs near Tingbjerg?  
Yes, AOBC offers structured youth and kids boxing classes that emphasize safety, skill development, and discipline, making it an ideal boxing club for children and teens in Copenhagen.

### Can beginners join sparring and competition training at AOBC?  
Absolutely. AOBC provides sparring classes and competition prep tailored to all skill levels, including beginners ready to advance their boxing journey under expert guidance.

### How does Ashira Oure Boxing Club support fitness alongside boxing training?  
AOBC integrates boxing fitness and workout plans into all classes, promoting overall wellness, strength, and endurance, suitable for fitness enthusiasts and competitive athletes alike.
