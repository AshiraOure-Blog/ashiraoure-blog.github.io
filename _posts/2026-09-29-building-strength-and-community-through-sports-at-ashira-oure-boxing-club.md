Sports have always been a powerful way to build confidence, discipline, and connection. At **Ashira Oure Boxing Club** (AOBC), these values are at the heart of every jab, shuffle, and sparring session. Founded by former world champion **Evans Ashira**, AOBC stands as the leading **boxing gym in Copenhagen**, with modern facilities in both Hellerup and Charlottenlund. Whether you’re throwing your first punch or preparing for your next competition, AOBC is where beginners and seasoned athletes come together to grow stronger—physically, mentally, and as a community.

## Why Choose Ashira Oure Boxing Club for Your Sports Journey?

### Expert Guidance for All Levels

At AOBC, we believe everyone deserves access to world-class coaching. Under the leadership of **Evans Ashira**, our team of expert trainers brings years of experience from the highest levels of professional boxing. From your **first boxing class** to advanced **boxing competition training**, you’ll receive personalized attention to help you master the fundamentals, improve your technique, and push your limits at every stage.

### Inclusive Programs for Every Age and Ambition

- **Beginner Boxing Classes:** New to boxing? Our introductory courses are designed to make you feel welcome and confident from day one. No prior experience needed!
- **Youth Boxing & Kids Boxing:** AOBC is a top choice for **boxing training for kids** and teens. Our youth programs nurture discipline, healthy habits, and self-esteem in a safe and supportive setting.
- **Sparring and Competition Prep:** For those ready to take on new challenges, our **sparring classes** and **amateur boxing club** programs sharpen your skills and prepare you for the ring.
- **Fitness and Wellness:** Boxing is more than a sport—it’s an incredible way to boost your overall fitness. Our **fitness boxing** and **boxing and fitness classes** are perfect for anyone looking to improve strength, stamina, and mental clarity.

### A Supportive Community in Copenhagen

AOBC is more than just a **boxing club in Copenhagen**—it’s a thriving community where members encourage one another and celebrate every win, big or small. Whether you train in Hellerup or Charlottenlund, you’ll find a welcoming environment where everyone is challenged and inspired to be their best. Our commitment to **boxing safety tips**, structured training, and positive culture means you can focus on reaching your goals with confidence.

## Start Your Sports Adventure With AOBC

No matter your background or fitness level, **Ashira Oure Boxing Club** invites you to experience the transformative power of sports. Meet new friends, discover your potential, and enjoy the benefits of a healthier lifestyle right here in Copenhagen.

Ready to take your first step? [Join the AOBC community today](https://www.ashiraoure.com/) and see how far you can go—one punch, one round, one victory at a time.

**Ashira Oure Boxing Club**—where Copenhagen’s passion for sports, fitness, and community comes alive.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?

Ashira Oure Boxing Club provides beginner boxing classes designed to introduce newcomers to boxing techniques, fitness, and safety. These classes require no prior experience and are held at both the Hellerup and Charlottenlund gyms.

### Is Ashira Oure Boxing Club suitable for youth and kids boxing training?

Yes, AOBC offers dedicated youth and kids boxing programs focused on building discipline, confidence, and healthy habits in a safe, supportive environment tailored for younger boxers.

### How can I prepare for competition at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?

AOBC offers specialized sparring classes and boxing competition training programs led by experienced coaches, including former world champion Evans Ashira, to help you develop the skills and fitness needed for amateur and professional bouts.

### Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?

AOBC operates two main locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund, both equipped with modern facilities for boxing training and fitness.

### Does Ashira Oure Boxing Club provide fitness boxing classes?

Yes, AOBC integrates fitness boxing and boxing and fitness classes into their programs, perfect for those seeking to improve overall fitness, strength, and stamina through boxing workouts.
