### A Community That Lifts Everyone in Hellerup and Charlottenlund

What sets Ashira Oure Boxing Club apart is its inclusive spirit. Whether you’re a total beginner, a fitness enthusiast, or an aspiring competitor, there’s a place for you here. Emil’s journey mirrors that of many members: starting out with **beginner boxing classes** and gradually progressing to sparring, fitness boxing, and even competition prep. At AOBC, no one trains alone. The camaraderie in both our **Hellerup boxing gym** and **Charlottenlund location** is undeniable, with members cheering each other on through every round.

### Training That Transforms: Boxing Fitness and Coaching

Emil’s routine at AOBC blends skill development, fitness, and fun:

- **Expert-led boxing classes** that break down the fundamentals  
- High-energy **boxing fitness** sessions  
- Safe and supportive **sparring classes**  
- Personal coaching tailored to individual goals  
- Access to youth and kids boxing programs, making AOBC a true family club  

Our coaches, including Evans Ashira, are dedicated to helping every member grow—whether that means mastering technique or simply becoming the healthiest, strongest version of yourself.

### Growth for All Levels: Boxing Training for Beginners to Competition Prep

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?**  
A1: AOBC offers beginner boxing classes designed to teach the fundamentals of boxing, focusing on skill, fitness, and discipline. These classes are ideal for newcomers looking to start their boxing journey in a supportive environment.

**Q2: Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?**  
A2: Ashira Oure Boxing Club has two main locations in the Copenhagen area: Hellerup and Charlottenlund, both equipped with expert coaching and excellent facilities for all levels.

**Q3: Does AOBC provide youth and kids boxing programs?**  
A3: Yes, Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers specialized youth and kids boxing programs that focus on skill development, fitness, and discipline, ensuring a fun and safe environment for younger athletes.

**Q4: Can I get personal coaching and competition training at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?**  
A4: Absolutely! AOBC provides personal coaching tailored to individual goals and offers comprehensive boxing competition training for amateur and aspiring competitive boxers.

**Q5: How does AOBC support fitness enthusiasts alongside boxers?**  
A5: AOBC blends boxing skill training with high-energy boxing fitness and sparring classes, making it an excellent choice for fitness enthusiasts seeking a dynamic workout that improves strength, endurance, and agility.
