## Training Through the Regn at Ashira Oure Boxing Club Copenhagen

Whether you’re stepping into Ashira Oure Boxing Club for your first beginner boxing class or preparing for your next big competition, the journey is about facing challenges and pushing through, just like training on a rainy day. Founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, AOBC is Copenhagen’s premier boxing gym—where determination is forged, and champions are made.

Our Hellerup and Charlottenlund locations welcome everyone, from absolute beginners to seasoned competitors. Rain or shine, our doors are open. Inside, you’ll find a vibrant boxing community that thrives on encouragement, discipline, and the shared goal of self-improvement.

## Why Choose AOBC, Rain or Shine? Your Local Boxing Gym in Copenhagen

- **Expert-Led Classes:** Every session at AOBC is guided by experienced coaches, including Evans Ashira himself. You’ll learn real boxing skills—whether you’re interested in fitness boxing, sparring classes, or boxing competition training.
- **All Levels Welcome:** We offer boxing classes for beginners, youth boxing programs, and advanced training for competitive fighters. Everyone finds their place here.
- **Fitness and Wellness:** Boxing at AOBC is more than just throwing punches. Our workouts enhance strength, agility, and mental toughness. Embrace boxing fitness and discover how regn can inspire you to keep moving forward.
- **Supportive Boxing Community in Copenhagen:** From your first boxing class to your hundredth, you’ll be surrounded by teammates who motivate and uplift each other—rainy days included.
