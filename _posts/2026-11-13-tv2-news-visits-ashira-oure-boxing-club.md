## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?**  
A1: AOBC provides beginner boxing classes tailored to all skill levels, focusing on fundamentals such as stance, punches, footwork, and fitness boxing. These classes are perfect for those new to boxing or looking to improve basic techniques in a supportive environment.

**Q2: Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?**  
A2: AOBC has two main locations in Copenhagen: Hellerup and Charlottenlund. Both gyms offer comprehensive boxing training, sparring classes, youth programs, and personal coaching to cater to all members.

**Q3: Does AOBC offer boxing training for kids and youth in Copenhagen?**  
A3: Yes, Ashira Oure Boxing Club has dedicated youth boxing programs and “boxing training for kids” that focus on skill development, fitness, discipline, and fun, making it an ideal “boxing club for beginners” of all ages.

**Q4: How can I prepare for boxing competitions at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?**  
A4: AOBC offers specialized boxing competition training led by former world champion Evans Ashira. The training includes advanced sparring, conditioning, boxing drills, and strategy sessions to prepare competitive and amateur boxers effectively.

**Q5: What makes Ashira Oure Boxing Club a top choice for boxing fitness in Copenhagen?**  
A5: AOBC combines authentic boxing techniques with fitness boxing and wellness programs. The club fosters a supportive boxing community, helping members improve their fitness, build confidence, and maintain boxing safety under expert guidance.
