At Ashira Oure Boxing Club, angreb—Danish for “attack”—is more than just a technique. It's a mindset that empowers boxers at every level, from first-timers to seasoned competitors, to step forward with confidence, discipline, and skill. Whether you're new to boxing in Copenhagen or hungry to sharpen your offensive game, AOBC is your home for learning, growth, and real results.

## The Art of Angreb in Boxing at AOBC Copenhagen

Angreb is about taking initiative. In the ring, it means knowing when to strike, how to combine speed and precision, and trusting in your training. At AOBC, founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, you’ll discover that angreb isn’t just about power—it’s about smart movement, timing, and strategy. Our boxing classes in Hellerup and Charlottenlund are designed to help you understand and master this essential part of boxing, regardless of your starting point.

### Why Angreb Matters for Every Boxer in Copenhagen

- **Beginners**: Learning angreb builds confidence, improves self-defense, and sets a strong foundation for all aspects of boxing.
- **Fitness Enthusiasts**: Offensive drills provide a dynamic, full-body workout that burns calories and boosts cardiovascular health.
- **Competitive Fighters**: Mastering angreb means controlling the tempo, outmaneuvering opponents, and turning defense into attack.
- **Youth Programs**: Kids and teens are taught angreb in a safe, supportive way, focusing on discipline, respect, and fun competition.

## AOBC’s Approach to Offensive Training in Hellerup & Charlottenlund

At Ashira Oure Boxing Club, offensive techniques are taught by expert coaches—including Evans Ashira himself—who tailor sessions to your goals and abilities. Our beginner boxing classes introduce the basics of angreb, while personal coaching and advanced courses break down complex combinations and fight tactics.

**AOBC’s angreb-focused training includes:**

- Step-by-step boxing drills for all levels
- Controlled sparring classes to practice angreb in real time
- Boxing competition training for those aiming to compete
- Boxing training for kids, ensuring safety and positive development

Safety is always our priority. We teach proper angreb form, follow boxing safety tips, and create a respectful environment where everyone can progress without pressure or judgment.

## Building Confidence Through the AOBC Boxing Community in Copenhagen

Joining AOBC means becoming part of a passionate boxing community in Copenhagen. Members support each other whether they’re working on their first jab or prepping for a title bout. Our culture is inclusive—everyone, from kids to adults, beginners to advanced athletes, is welcomed and encouraged.

We know that the journey to mastering angreb, and boxing as a whole, is unique for every person. That’s why our coaches—including Evans Ashira, Yuna Ashira, and Lucas Ashira—are committed to helping you set goals, overcome challenges, and celebrate every victory, big or small.

## Ready to Step Forward with Ashira Oure Boxing Club?

Whether you want your first boxing class, to join our youth boxing program, or to push your limits as a competitor, we have a place for you at Ashira Oure Boxing Club.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?
Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers beginner boxing classes in both Hellerup and Charlottenlund, designed to teach the fundamentals of boxing, including angreb, with a focus on safety, technique, and building confidence.

### How can I join youth boxing programs at AOBC?
AOBC provides youth boxing classes tailored for kids and teens, focusing on skill development, discipline, and fun in a supportive environment. You can register through their website or visit the gym locations in Copenhagen.

### Does AOBC provide sparring classes and competition training?
Yes, Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers controlled sparring classes and specialized boxing competition training to help both amateur and competitive boxers prepare effectively for bouts.

### Who founded Ashira Oure Boxing Club and what is their expertise?
AOBC was founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, who brings extensive professional experience and coaching expertise to develop boxers of all levels in Copenhagen.

### Are there boxing and fitness classes available at AOBC?
Yes, AOBC combines boxing drills with fitness workouts to provide dynamic boxing fitness classes that improve cardiovascular health, strength, and overall endurance.
