## How Boxing Training Elevates Your Hyrox Performance in Copenhagen

Boxing is about more than punches; it’s about movement, coordination, and resilience—all crucial for hyrox. Here’s how training at our Copenhagen boxing gym gives you an edge:

- **Stamina and Endurance:** Our boxing workout plans and group classes push your cardiovascular limits, prepping you for the relentless pace of hyrox.
- **Strength and Agility:** Boxing drills develop explosive power, core strength, and quick footwork—helping you transition seamlessly between running and workout stations.
- **Mental Toughness:** The focus and discipline honed through boxing help you stay sharp and determined, even as the competition heats up.
