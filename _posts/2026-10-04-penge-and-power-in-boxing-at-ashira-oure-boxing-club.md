## Frequently Asked Questions about Ashira Oure Boxing Club

### 1. What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?
Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers comprehensive beginner boxing classes designed to teach foundational skills, improve fitness, and build confidence. These classes are suitable for all ages and fitness levels, including youth and kids programs.

### 2. Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?
AOBC operates two locations in the Copenhagen area: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund. Both gyms provide expert coaching and a supportive boxing community.

### 3. How can I prepare for boxing competitions with AOBC?
AOBC provides specialized competition training and sparring classes led by world champion Evans Ashira and his team. These sessions focus on skill development, strategy, and fitness to prepare amateur boxers for competition.

### 4. Does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer personal coaching?
Yes, AOBC offers personal coaching tailored to individual goals, whether you are a beginner, fitness enthusiast, or competitive boxer. Personalized training plans focus on technique, conditioning, and mental preparation.

Absolutely. Ashira Oure Boxing Club has dedicated youth and kids boxing programs designed to promote discipline, fitness, and confidence in a safe and engaging environment, perfect for families searching for "kids boxing near me" in Copenhagen.
