## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?

Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers beginner boxing classes designed to teach the fundamentals of boxing, including footwork, technique, and fitness. These classes cater to all ages and skill levels, making it an ideal boxing club for beginners in Copenhagen.

### Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?

AOBC has two premier locations in Copenhagen—Hellerup and Charlottenlund—making it accessible for residents across the city looking for a professional boxing gym.

### Does AOBC provide youth and kids boxing programs?

Yes, AOBC offers structured youth boxing and boxing training for kids, focusing on skill development, fitness, and discipline in a supportive environment, perfect for families searching for "kids boxing near me."

### Can I prepare for boxing competitions at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?

Absolutely. AOBC provides specialized boxing competition training and sparring classes tailored to aspiring competitors and experienced fighters, with expert coaching from Evans Ashira and the team.

### What makes Ashira Oure Boxing Club unique compared to other boxing gyms in Copenhagen?

AOBC stands out due to its expert-led training, inclusive community atmosphere, comprehensive programs for all levels, and the legacy of founder Evans Ashira, a former world champion. The club combines skill, fitness, and discipline in a supportive environment.
