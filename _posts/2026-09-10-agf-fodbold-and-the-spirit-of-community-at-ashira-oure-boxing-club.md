At Ashira Oure Boxing Club, the energy and unity seen in AGF fodbold are more than just inspiration—they’re part of our DNA. In Copenhagen, where sports bring people together, AOBC stands out as a boxing club where passion for fitness, discipline, and camaraderie thrive.

## Boxing and Football: A Shared Commitment to Excellence in Copenhagen

Just as AGF fodbold unites fans and players with a common goal, Ashira Oure Boxing Club brings together people from all walks of life across Copenhagen and its suburbs like Hellerup and Charlottenlund. Whether you’re a beginner stepping into your first boxing class, a youth eager to build confidence, or a competitive boxer preparing for your next match, our club welcomes you with open arms.

Founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, AOBC is a leading boxing gym in Copenhagen, with convenient locations in Hellerup boxing gym and Charlottenlund. Our mission is to help every member reach their potential—physically, mentally, and as part of a supportive community.

## Why Ashira Oure Boxing Club Is Copenhagen’s Premier Boxing Gym

- **Expert Leadership by Evans Ashira:** Train under Evans Ashira, whose global experience elevates every session, from beginner boxing classes to advanced boxing competition training.
- **Inclusive Boxing Classes and Youth Programs:** We offer boxing classes for all levels and ages, including kids boxing and youth boxing programs, making us a true community boxing academy and bokseklub København.
- **Fitness and Wellness Focus:** Enjoy a diverse mix of boxing fitness, sparring classes, and personal coaching tailored to your needs. Whether you’re looking for boxing and fitness classes, a boxing workout plan, or guidance on how to train for boxing, AOBC delivers.
- **Community and Discipline:** Like the best football clubs, AOBC is built on respect, discipline, and teamwork. Members encourage each other, celebrate progress, and push one another to achieve more—inside and outside the ring.
- **Safety and Support:** From your first boxing class to your first sparring session, our coaches emphasize boxing safety tips and ensure a positive environment for growth.

## For Beginners and Champions Alike in Copenhagen

Are you new to boxing and searching for a “boxing club for beginners” or “beginner boxing classes” in Copenhagen? AOBC is the perfect place to start. Our supportive coaches break down boxing drills and boxing sparring rules, teach the basics, and help you build strength, confidence, and skill with every session.

For competitive athletes, our boxing competition training is second to none. Join sparring classes, refine your technique, and prepare for tournaments with expert guidance. AOBC’s dedication to every member’s journey sets us apart from other clubs—whether you’re training for fitness boxing or aiming for the amateur boxing club circuit.

## Youth Programs Building Future Champions in Hellerup and Charlottenlund

Youth development is at the heart of Ashira Oure Boxing Club. Our youth boxing and kids boxing classes in Hellerup and Charlottenlund are designed to instill discipline, confidence, and a lifelong love of sport. Like AGF fodbold’s commitment to nurturing young talent, AOBC provides a safe, fun, and motivating environment for young boxers to grow.

## Join the Boxing Community and Gym with Boxing Focus in Copenhagen

Ashira Oure Boxing Club is more than just a gym—it’s a community united by passion, discipline, and the drive to improve. If you’re inspired by the spirit of AGF fodbold and want to experience the camaraderie and challenge of boxing, AOBC welcomes you.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What types of boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer for beginners?**  
A1: AOBC offers beginner boxing classes designed to teach boxing fundamentals, boxing drills, and boxing safety tips. These classes help newcomers build confidence and skill in a supportive environment in Copenhagen, including Hellerup and Charlottenlund locations.

**Q2: Does AOBC provide youth and kids boxing programs?**  
A2: Yes, Ashira Oure Boxing Club specializes in youth boxing and kids boxing classes that focus on discipline, fitness, and personal growth. These programs are ideal for young athletes seeking a safe and motivating boxing academy in Copenhagen.

**Q3: How can I prepare for boxing competitions at AOBC?**  
A3: AOBC offers expert boxing competition training, including sparring classes and personalized coaching by former world champion Evans Ashira. The training covers technique refinement, sparring rules, and competition readiness tailored to amateurs and competitive boxers.

**Q4: Where are the Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?**  
A4: AOBC has two main locations: a boxing gym in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund, serving Copenhagen and surrounding areas with comprehensive boxing and fitness classes.

**Q5: What makes AOBC different from other boxing gyms in Copenhagen?**  
A5: AOBC combines expert leadership, inclusive programs, a strong community focus, and an emphasis on fitness and wellness. Founded by Evans Ashira, it provides a unique boxing community experience for beginners, youth, and competitive boxers alike.
