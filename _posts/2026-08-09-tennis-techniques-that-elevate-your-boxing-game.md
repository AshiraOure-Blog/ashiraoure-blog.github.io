## The Power of Crossover Training in Copenhagen Boxing Club

Tennis and boxing may seem worlds apart, but both sports demand lightning-fast footwork, sharp hand-eye coordination, and relentless endurance. Integrating tennis-inspired drills into your boxing classes or fitness workouts at AOBC can unlock new levels of performance, whether you’re training in Hellerup, Charlottenlund, or beyond.

### Why Tennis Techniques Work for Boxers at Ashira Oure Boxing Club

- **Agility and Footwork:** Tennis players pivot, lunge, and sidestep with precision—just like advanced boxers. Practicing tennis footwork drills can enhance your ring movement, making you a more elusive and effective fighter.
- **Explosive Power:** Swinging a racket and throwing a punch both require core strength and rotational power. Tennis-inspired medicine ball throws or resistance band exercises can help you develop knockout force.
- **Endurance:** Long rallies in tennis mirror the sustained intensity of sparring classes or competition training. Interval drills from tennis can boost your stamina for those final rounds in the ring.
