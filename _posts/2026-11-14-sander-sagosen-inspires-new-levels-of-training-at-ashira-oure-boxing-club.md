## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What types of boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer for beginners in Copenhagen?  
Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers beginner boxing classes tailored to newcomers, focusing on fundamentals, fitness, and building confidence in a supportive environment. These classes are ideal for those new to boxing looking for a welcoming Copenhagen boxing gym.

### Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?  
AOBC has two convenient locations in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, making it accessible for residents across the Copenhagen area seeking quality boxing training and community.

### Can kids and youth join boxing training programs at AOBC?  
Yes, AOBC provides specialized youth boxing and kids boxing programs designed to teach discipline, fitness, and boxing skills in a safe and encouraging setting, perfect for young beginners and aspiring athletes.

### Who founded Ashira Oure Boxing Club and what is their background?  
Ashira Oure Boxing Club was founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, who brings extensive international experience and coaching expertise to the club, ensuring high-quality training for all members.

### Does AOBC offer sparring and competition training for more advanced boxers?  
Absolutely. AOBC provides advanced sparring classes and boxing competition training tailored for amateur and competitive boxers aiming to improve their skills and prepare for fights.
