### Frequently Asked Questions About Ashira Oure Boxing Club

**Q1: What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?**  
AOBC offers beginner boxing classes tailored for all ages, including youth and kids programs. These classes focus on basic techniques, fitness boxing, and building confidence in a supportive environment.

**Q2: Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?**  
AOBC operates two main gyms located in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, serving Copenhagen and nearby areas with comprehensive boxing and fitness training.

**Q3: Can I prepare for boxing competitions at AOBC?**

**Q4: Is there boxing training available for kids and youth at AOBC?**

**Q5: How can I join my first boxing class at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?**
