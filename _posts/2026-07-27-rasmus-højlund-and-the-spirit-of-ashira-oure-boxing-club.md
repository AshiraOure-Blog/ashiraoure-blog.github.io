If you follow Danish sports, you know the name Rasmus Højlund—a young football star whose dedication, work ethic, and ambition have inspired athletes across Copenhagen. At Ashira Oure Boxing Club (AOBC), we see the same drive every day. Whether you’re a beginner stepping into the ring for the first time or a competitor training for your next big bout, the spirit that propels Højlund forward is alive in our boxing gym.

### A Community That Champions Every Member in Copenhagen and Surrounding Areas

Ashira Oure Boxing Club in Hellerup and Charlottenlund isn’t just a place to learn punches. Founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, AOBC is a community built on support, discipline, and a relentless commitment to growth. Here, every member—no matter their experience—finds a welcoming atmosphere and expert coaching.

- **Beginners:** Our beginner boxing classes and boxing club for beginners in Copenhagen are designed for anyone ready to try something new. We believe everyone, from all walks of life, can discover their own strength through boxing.
- **Youth and Kids:** AOBC offers youth boxing and boxing training for kids, providing a safe and inspiring space for young athletes to thrive. Looking for kids boxing near me? Our coaches, including Yuna Ashira, nurture confidence and discipline in every session.
- **Fitness Enthusiasts:** Not every member aspires to compete—many join for fitness boxing and to experience the energizing workouts unique to a top boxing club Copenhagen offers. Our fitness-focused boxing classes help you build stamina, burn calories, and boost mental resilience.
- **Competitive Boxers:** For those ready to test their skills, we offer sparring classes, boxing competition training, and personal coaching. The same attention to detail that made Evans Ashira a world champion is at the core of AOBC’s training philosophy.

### Inspired by Dedication and Discipline at Ashira Oure Boxing Club Copenhagen

Rasmus Højlund’s rise in football highlights the value of dedication, teamwork, and pushing through challenges. At AOBC, these values are woven into our daily training. Our coaches, including Evans, Yuna, Lucas, and Mino Ashira, emphasize structured boxing workout plans, boxing sparring rules, safety tips, and the discipline that sets great athletes apart. 

From your first boxing class to advanced sparring, you’ll learn:

- Proper technique and footwork
- Boxing drills that enhance coordination
- How to train for boxing safely and effectively
- The importance of community—training with others who share your ambition

### Why AOBC Is Copenhagen’s Premier Boxing Academy and Gym with Boxing

- **Expert-Led Training:** Benefit from world-class coaching by Evans Ashira and our experienced team.
- **Flexible Programs:** Choose from boxing and fitness classes, amateur boxing club sessions, or personalized coaching.
- **Supportive Atmosphere:** AOBC is more than a gym with boxing—it’s a place to grow, find motivation, and connect with others.
- **Regional Roots:** Proudly serving Copenhagen, Hellerup, and Charlottenlund, we’re your go-to boxing gym Copenhagen locals trust.

### Join the AOBC Movement in Copenhagen and Hellerup

If you’re inspired by the determination of athletes like Rasmus Højlund, there’s a place for you at Ashira Oure Boxing Club. Whether you’re new to boxing or aiming for the next level, we’re here to support your journey—step by step, punch by punch.

Ready to take the first step? [Discover our classes and become part of Copenhagen’s leading boxing community at AOBC.](https://www.ashiraoure.com/)

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What types of beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?**  
A1: AOBC provides beginner boxing classes specifically designed for newcomers to learn the fundamentals, including proper technique, footwork, and basic boxing drills, ensuring a safe and supportive environment for all skill levels.

**Q2: Does AOBC offer boxing training for kids and youth in Copenhagen?**  
A2: Yes, Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers youth boxing and boxing training for kids, with experienced coaches like Yuna Ashira focusing on building confidence, discipline, and skills in a safe setting.

**Q3: Can I combine boxing with fitness training at AOBC?**  
A3: Absolutely. AOBC features boxing and fitness classes tailored for fitness enthusiasts who want to improve stamina, burn calories, and enjoy the benefits of boxing fitness in a motivating community.

**Q4: Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s gyms located?**  
A4: AOBC has two convenient locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund, serving the greater Copenhagen area with top-tier boxing training.

**Q5: What competitive boxing programs does AOBC provide?**  
A5: For competitive boxers, AOBC offers sparring classes, boxing competition training, and personalized coaching led by former world champion Evans Ashira, preparing athletes for amateur and professional bouts.
