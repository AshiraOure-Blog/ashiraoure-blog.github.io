- **Personal Coaching:** Tailored plans help you reach individual goals, whether you’re preparing for your first boxing class or your next tournament.
- **Safe, Supportive Community:** AOBC prioritizes *boxing safety tips* and a team atmosphere where everyone lifts each other up.

We nurture discipline, resilience, and self-belief—values at the core of both boxing and broader wellness. Our *boxing academy* and *amateur boxing club* programs are designed to challenge seasoned athletes while guiding newcomers step by step.

For young athletes and families, our *boxing training for kids* and *kids boxing near me* options create a safe, encouraging space to learn skills, build friendships, and develop healthy habits.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What types of boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer for beginners?**  
A1: AOBC offers beginner boxing classes tailored to newcomers, focusing on fundamental boxing techniques, fitness, and safety. These classes provide a supportive environment to build confidence and skills step by step.

**Q2: Where are the Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?**  
A2: Ashira Oure Boxing Club has two convenient locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund, making it accessible for residents across the city and surrounding areas.

**Q3: Can children join boxing training programs at AOBC?**  
A3: Yes, AOBC provides specialized boxing training for kids and youth programs designed to teach boxing fundamentals, discipline, and fitness in a safe, encouraging environment.

**Q4: Who is Evans Ashira, and what role does he play at AOBC?**  
A4: Evans Ashira is a former world boxing champion and the founder of Ashira Oure Boxing Club. He leads expert coaching sessions and helps foster a high-performance yet supportive community at AOBC.

**Q5: Does AOBC provide sparring and competition training for advanced boxers?**  
A5: Absolutely. AOBC offers dedicated sparring classes and boxing competition training to prepare amateur and competitive boxers for tournaments, ensuring they develop both skills and strategic experience.
