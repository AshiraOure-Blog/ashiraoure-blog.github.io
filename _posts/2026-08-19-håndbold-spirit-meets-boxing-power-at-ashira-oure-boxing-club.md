## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What types of boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer for beginners in Copenhagen?

Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers beginner boxing classes tailored to all fitness levels. These classes focus on fundamental skills, boxing drills, and conditioning to help newcomers build confidence and technique in a supportive environment.

### Where are the Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?

AOBC has two convenient locations in Copenhagen: Hellerup and Charlottenlund. Both gyms provide expert coaching, sparring classes, and youth boxing programs to serve the local community.

### Does AOBC provide youth and kids boxing training programs?

Yes, AOBC offers structured youth and kids boxing programs designed to teach discipline, fitness, and boxing fundamentals safely. These programs are ideal for children and teens looking for a positive, energetic boxing community near them.

### How does Ashira Oure Boxing Club support competitive boxers preparing for tournaments?

AOBC provides advanced sparring classes and boxing competition training designed to improve technique, endurance, and ring strategy. Personalized coaching from former world champion Evans Ashira helps athletes prepare effectively for amateur boxing competitions.

### Is Ashira Oure Boxing Club suitable for fitness enthusiasts looking for boxing workouts?

Absolutely. AOBC combines boxing and fitness classes to enhance strength, agility, and overall conditioning. Their boxing fitness and workout plans cater to fitness enthusiasts seeking an engaging, full-body exercise routine.
