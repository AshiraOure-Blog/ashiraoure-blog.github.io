The energy of CPH Half pulses through Copenhagen every year, uniting runners, athletes, and fitness enthusiasts in a celebration of endurance and community. At Ashira Oure Boxing Club (AOBC), we know that spirit well. Whether you’re chasing your first finish line or striving for a personal best in or out of the ring, our boxing gym in Hellerup and Charlottenlund welcomes you to harness that same drive and determination.

## Boxing Training That Matches Your Ambition in Copenhagen

Just as the CPH Half draws participants from all walks of life, AOBC is a boxing club for everyone in Copenhagen and the surrounding areas. Founded by former world champion **Evans Ashira**, our community thrives on inclusivity, energy, and motivation. From absolute beginners to seasoned competitors, you’ll find your place here:

- **Beginner Boxing Classes:** Perfect for those new to boxing or looking to boost their fitness routine. No experience required—just bring your enthusiasm!
- **Boxing and Fitness Classes:** Dynamic workouts designed to build strength, stamina, and skill. Ideal for runners, cross-trainers, and anyone looking to level up their fitness.
- **Sparring Classes:** For those seeking a challenge, our expert-led sparring sessions develop tactical thinking and resilience.
- **Personal Coaching:** One-on-one training tailored to your goals, whether it’s competition prep or holistic wellness.
- **Youth Boxing and Kids Boxing:** AOBC is proud to offer youth programs that foster discipline and confidence through age-appropriate boxing training.

## A Supportive Boxing Community in Copenhagen

Completing the CPH Half is about more than just crossing the finish line—it’s about community, perseverance, and growth. At **Ashira Oure Boxing Club**, you’ll discover a supportive environment where members uplift each other, celebrate progress, and push beyond limits. Our coaches, including Evans Ashira, Lucas Ashira, and Yuna Ashira, are passionate about helping every individual find their strength, both inside and outside the ring.

## Why Runners and Fitness Enthusiasts Love Boxing Training at AOBC

Boxing and running share more than you might think. Both require discipline, endurance, and mental focus. Many runners at the CPH Half have discovered that cross-training with boxing improves core strength, coordination, and overall athletic performance. Whether you’re preparing for your first race or your next bout, our **boxing fitness** programs offer the perfect complement to your training schedule.

### Benefits of Joining AOBC in Copenhagen:

- **Build full-body strength and cardiovascular endurance**
- **Improve balance, agility, and reaction time**
- **Boost mental resilience and self-confidence**
- **Join a welcoming Copenhagen boxing community**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What types of boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer for beginners?
AOBC offers beginner boxing classes that require no prior experience. These classes focus on basic skills, fitness, and building confidence, making them ideal for anyone new to boxing.

### Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?
Ashira Oure Boxing Club operates gyms in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, serving Copenhagen and nearby areas with top-tier boxing training and fitness programs.

### Can kids and youth join boxing programs at AOBC?
Yes, AOBC provides specialized youth and kids boxing programs designed to promote discipline, confidence, and age-appropriate boxing skills in a safe environment.

### How can boxing training at AOBC benefit runners and fitness enthusiasts?
Boxing training improves core strength, endurance, coordination, and mental resilience, making it an excellent cross-training option for runners and fitness enthusiasts preparing for events like the CPH Half.

### Does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer personal coaching and competition preparation?
Yes, AOBC offers personalized coaching tailored to individual goals, including sparring, competition training, and holistic fitness development led by experienced coaches like Evans Ashira.
