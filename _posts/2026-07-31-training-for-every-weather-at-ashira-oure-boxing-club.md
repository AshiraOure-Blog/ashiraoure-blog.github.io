When the Copenhagen sky turns grey and the wind picks up over Hellerup and Charlottenlund, motivation can dip. But at Ashira Oure Boxing Club, the weather is never an excuse—it’s an invitation. Whether it’s raining, snowing, or the sun is shining, AOBC is the boxing gym Copenhagen residents rely on to keep their spirits high and their fitness on track.

#### Why Train at AOBC No Matter the Weather?

Ashira Oure Boxing Club, founded by world champion Evans Ashira, is not just a place to escape the cold. It’s a year-round hub for skill development, fitness, and community. From beginner boxing classes to professional competition training, AOBC welcomes everyone—kids, teens, adults, and seasoned competitors. When outdoor motivation wanes, our supportive community and expert coaches help you stay committed to your boxing and fitness goals.

#### How AOBC Turns Weather Into a Training Advantage

In Copenhagen, the weather can be unpredictable. At AOBC, we make it our strength:

- **Indoor, All-Weather Training:** Our modern facilities in Hellerup and Charlottenlund mean your boxing workout plan is never interrupted. Rain or shine, you can rely on a consistent training schedule.
- **Motivating Community:** When the weather outside is gloomy, the warmth and energy inside AOBC lift everyone. Our community is built on encouragement, discipline, and shared progress.
- **Expert-Led Classes:** Led by Evans Ashira and a team of passionate coaches—including youth programs, fitness boxing, sparring classes, and personal coaching—there’s a place for every level and age.
- **Boxing Fitness for All:** AOBC is the boxing club Copenhagen trusts for both boxing and fitness. Whether you’re here for boxing fitness, amateur boxing, or competition training, you’ll find tailored programs and a welcoming environment.

#### Weatherproof Your Fitness Routine

Don’t let the Danish climate hold you back. AOBC’s structured boxing and fitness classes keep you active and engaged, no matter what’s happening outside. For beginners, our first boxing class is a supportive introduction, with expert safety tips and step-by-step guidance. For competitive fighters, our boxing sparring rules and advanced drills keep your skills sharp all year.

#### For the Next Generation: Youth Boxing at AOBC

Kids and teens need a positive outlet—especially when it’s wet or cold outside. Ashira Oure Boxing Club offers youth boxing and kids boxing near you, designed to build discipline, confidence, and lifelong fitness habits. Our programs (including those led by Yuna Ashira and Lucas Ashira) make AOBC the amateur boxing club families trust.

#### Join Copenhagen’s Boxing Community—Whatever the Weather

Whether you’re a beginner, a fitness enthusiast, or a competitive boxer, Ashira Oure Boxing Club is your destination to grow stronger, stay motivated, and connect with a vibrant community. Let the weather inspire you to step inside, try something new, and see how far you can go.

Ready to train with us? [Book your first class at AOBC today!](https://www.ashiraoure.com/)

Ashira Oure Boxing Club stands strong through every season—helping Copenhagen stay fit, focused, and inspired, whatever the weather.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?**  
A1: AOBC offers beginner boxing classes designed for all ages and fitness levels. These classes include step-by-step instruction, safety tips, and a welcoming environment to help newcomers learn boxing fundamentals in Copenhagen.

**Q2: Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?**  
A2: Ashira Oure Boxing Club has two convenient locations in the Copenhagen area: Hellerup and Charlottenlund, providing easy access to quality boxing training regardless of weather conditions.

**Q3: Does AOBC provide youth boxing programs for kids in Copenhagen?**  
A3: Yes, AOBC offers specialized youth boxing programs led by expert coaches, including Yuna Ashira and Lucas Ashira. These classes focus on building discipline, confidence, and fitness for kids and teens.

**Q4: Can I train for boxing competitions at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?**  
A4: Absolutely. AOBC provides advanced boxing competition training, sparring classes, and tailored workout plans to prepare amateur and competitive boxers for fights and tournaments.

**Q5: How does AOBC accommodate training during Copenhagen’s unpredictable weather?**  
A5: With indoor facilities in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, AOBC ensures that your boxing and fitness routines continue uninterrupted year-round, regardless of rain, snow, or cold weather.
