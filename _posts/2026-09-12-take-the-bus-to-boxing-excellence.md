### What beginner boxing classes are available at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?

AOBC offers tailored beginner boxing classes designed for those new to the sport. These classes focus on fundamental skills, fitness, and building confidence in a supportive environment.

### Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?

AOBC has two convenient locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund, both easily accessible by bus.
