## Building Endurance for CPH Half Through Boxing in Copenhagen

Training for the CPH Half requires not only strong legs but also a resilient mind and body. Boxing offers a unique way to complement your running routine:

- **Full-Body Conditioning:** Our boxing workouts at Ashira Oure focus on agility, balance, and stamina—skills essential for long-distance events like the CPH Half.
- **Core Strength:** Punching, footwork, and defensive drills build a powerful core, enhancing your running efficiency.
- **Mental Toughness:** The discipline and focus you develop in the ring are just as valuable on the road.

- **Beginner Boxing Classes:** No experience? No problem. Our coaches guide you through your first boxing class with patience and expertise.
- **Advanced Sparring and Competition Prep:** For those looking to push their limits, we offer sparring classes and boxing competition training under the guidance of seasoned professionals.
- **Youth and Kids Boxing:** Our youth boxing and kids boxing near me programs introduce young athletes to the sport in a fun, safe, and disciplined environment.

## Fitness Boxing and Boxing Training for Beginners: The Perfect Cross-Training

Boxing and fitness go hand in hand. Many runners preparing for the CPH Half find that joining a boxing club for beginners adds a dynamic edge to their training schedule. At AOBC, our boxing fitness classes are designed to improve cardiovascular health, build lean muscle, and keep workouts exciting.

**Benefits of cross-training with boxing:**
- Reduces risk of overuse injuries
- Improves overall athletic performance
- Keeps motivation high with varied, engaging workouts
