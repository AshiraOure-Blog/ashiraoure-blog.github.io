## Building Champions at Ashira Oure Boxing Club in Copenhagen

Founded by world champion Evans Ashira, Ashira Oure Boxing Club stands as Copenhagen’s premier boxing gym, with locations in Hellerup and Charlottenlund. We welcome everyone, from complete beginners to seasoned competitors. Our club’s focus on skill development, fitness, and the supportive spirit of our boxing community ensures you’ll always have a place to grow.

**What makes AOBC unique?**

- **Expert-Led Training:** Our coaches, including Evans Ashira himself, bring world-class experience from the ring to every class and private session.
- **All-Level Classes:** Whether you’re searching for beginner boxing classes, sparring sessions, or boxing competition training, we offer programs for every level—youth, adults, and advanced fighters.
- **Community Spirit:** AOBC is more than just a boxing gym in Copenhagen. It’s a supportive environment where members encourage each other, share successes, and celebrate progress.
- **Holistic Wellness:** We integrate boxing fitness, discipline, and mental resilience, helping you build a winning mindset—just like Johannes Hoff Thorup.

- **Personal Coaching:** Train one-on-one with experienced coaches to refine your technique and develop a personalized boxing workout plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What types of boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer for beginners?**  
A1: AOBC offers beginner boxing classes tailored to help newcomers learn the fundamentals of boxing safely and effectively. These classes focus on basic techniques, boxing drills, and building fitness in a supportive environment.

**Q2: Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?**  
A2: Ashira Oure Boxing Club has two convenient locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund, providing easy access for members across the city.

**Q3: Does AOBC provide youth and kids boxing programs?**  
A3: Yes, AOBC offers specialized youth boxing classes designed to teach children boxing skills, discipline, and fitness in a safe and encouraging setting — perfect for families searching for "kids boxing near me."

**Q4: Can I get personal coaching and competition training at AOBC?**  
A4: Absolutely. Ashira Oure Boxing Club provides personal coaching and sparring classes to prepare members for amateur boxing competitions, helping you refine techniques and develop a personalized boxing training schedule.

**Q5: How does Ashira Oure Boxing Club support overall fitness and wellness?**  
A5: Beyond boxing skills, AOBC integrates fitness boxing and wellness routines that enhance stamina, strength, and mental resilience, fostering a holistic approach to health in the supportive AOBC community.
