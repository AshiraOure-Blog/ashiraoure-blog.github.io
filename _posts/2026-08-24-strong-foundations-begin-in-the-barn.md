Here, every punch, every drill, and every class is a step toward personal excellence.

No matter your age or experience, AOBC stands as Copenhagen’s premier boxing club for everyone. Our beginner boxing classes offer the perfect introduction, led by expert coaches who emphasize technique, safety, and fun. For those looking to test their skills, our sparring classes and boxing competition training provide a structured path to achievement.

**At AOBC, you’ll find:**
- **Youth boxing and kids boxing near me:** Engaging programs for young boxers to build confidence and discipline.
- **Fitness boxing:** Challenging workouts that improve stamina, strength, and agility.
- **Personal coaching:** Tailored sessions for all ages and goals.
- **A supportive boxing community:** Train alongside friends, family, and future champions.

### Building Champions in Copenhagen with Ashira Boxing

Our boxing club in Copenhagen goes beyond workouts—it’s about building character, resilience, and lifelong friendships. Whether you’re drawn to the barn for fitness, competition, or self-discovery, AOBC champions your growth every step of the way.

A1: AOBC offers beginner boxing classes designed for all ages and skill levels. Our expert coaches focus on teaching proper technique, safety, and building confidence, making it ideal for anyone new to boxing in Copenhagen.

**Q2: Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club’s locations in Copenhagen?**  
A2: Ashira Oure Boxing Club has two main locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund, both equipped with top-tier facilities for boxing training.

**Q3: Does AOBC provide boxing training programs for youth and kids?**  
A3: Yes, AOBC offers specialized youth boxing and kids boxing classes that focus on discipline, fitness, and skill development in a safe and supportive environment.

**Q4: What types of boxing training and classes can I find at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?**

**Q5: Who is Evans Ashira and what role does he play at AOBC?**
