## Join the Premier Boxing Club Copenhagen Trusts

Founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, Ashira Oure Boxing Club (AOBC) stands as a beacon for those seeking skill, fitness, and personal growth through boxing. With locations in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, AOBC is more than a boxing gym in Copenhagen—it’s a place where discipline, camaraderie, and individual transformation come together.

Whether you’re inspired by the fierce competition between Denmark and Wales or simply looking for a new challenge, our doors are open to all. Here’s why AOBC is the perfect place for your boxing journey:

### All Levels Welcome

- **Beginner Boxing Classes:** Curious about boxing but not sure where to start? AOBC offers beginner-friendly classes tailored to help you build confidence, learn technique, and get fit in a supportive setting.
- **Youth Boxing and Kids Programs:** Looking for boxing training for kids or youth boxing in Copenhagen? Our experienced coaches ensure young athletes learn discipline, teamwork, and self-mastery in a safe, encouraging environment.
- **Sparring and Competition Preparation:** Ready to test your skills? Our sparring classes and competition training are designed for those aiming to step into the ring, guided by AOBC’s expert trainers.

### World-Class Coaching

Evans Ashira brings international experience and a champion’s mindset to every session. Supported by a passionate team—including Yuna Ashira and Lucas Ashira—our coaching staff is dedicated to helping you achieve your goals, whether you aim for fitness, self-defense, or the thrill of amateur boxing competition.

### Fitness, Wellness, and Community

At AOBC, we believe boxing is for everyone. Our boxing and fitness classes blend technique with dynamic workouts, supporting your health and wellness journey. Members experience:

- Structured boxing workout plans
- Expert guidance on boxing safety tips
- A warm, inclusive boxing community

No matter your age or experience, you’ll find a place at AOBC to grow stronger—in body, mind, and spirit.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?  
Ashira Oure Boxing Club provides beginner boxing classes designed to build confidence, teach fundamental techniques, and improve fitness in a supportive environment, making it ideal for newcomers to boxing in Copenhagen.

### Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?  
AOBC has two prime locations in Copenhagen: Hellerup and Charlottenlund, conveniently serving residents seeking a boxing gym in the greater Copenhagen area.

### Does AOBC offer boxing training programs for kids and youth?  
Yes, AOBC offers specialized youth boxing and kids boxing programs focused on discipline, teamwork, and skill development in a safe and encouraging setting.

### Who leads the coaching team at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?  
The coaching team is led by former world champion Evans Ashira, supported by his expert staff including Yuna Ashira and Lucas Ashira, ensuring world-class training for all members.

### Can I prepare for boxing competitions at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?  
Absolutely. AOBC offers sparring classes and boxing competition training designed to prepare amateur boxers for competitive matches under professional guidance.
