In 2026, Copenhagen will be alive with celebration as the city comes together for Pride 2026. At Ashira Oure Boxing Club (AOBC), we’re thrilled to stand at the heart of this spirit—championing inclusion, empowerment, and the power of community. Whether you’re just starting your boxing journey or you’re already a seasoned competitor, AOBC is where everyone is welcome to find strength, wellness, and pride in their progress.

## A Boxing Community Built on Pride and Progress in Copenhagen

Founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, AOBC has become a leading boxing gym in Copenhagen, with locations in Hellerup and Charlottenlund. Our vision is clear: to create a space where all individuals—regardless of background, experience, or fitness level—can come together, push their limits, and celebrate their achievements.

From beginner boxing classes to youth boxing programs, sparring sessions, and competition training, AOBC offers something for everyone in the Copenhagen area:

- **Beginner Boxing Classes:** Step into the ring with confidence at AOBC. Our expert coaches, including Evans Ashira and his team, guide you through fundamental techniques, boxing drills, and fitness routines designed for all abilities.
- **Fitness Boxing and Group Workouts:** Experience high-energy, supportive classes that blend boxing fitness with strength and cardio for a full-body workout.
- **Personal Coaching:** Tailored one-on-one sessions help you set goals—whether it’s mastering the basics or preparing for your next bout.
- **Youth and Kids Boxing:** Our youth boxing and kids boxing near me programs empower young boxers in Copenhagen to build discipline, resilience, and self-esteem in a safe, structured environment.
- **Sparring and Competition Prep:** For those ready to take it further, our sparring classes and boxing competition training offer a chance to test skills and prepare for amateur or competitive boxing.

## Why AOBC is Copenhagen’s Premier Boxing Club and Gym

When you walk through the doors of Ashira Oure Boxing Club, you’re joining much more than a gym. You’re becoming part of a vibrant boxing community rooted in respect, encouragement, and the shared pursuit of excellence.

- **Expert-Led Training:** With a legacy built by Evans Ashira, AOBC’s coaching team brings world-class expertise to every session.
- **Focus on Boxing Fitness and Wellness:** Our programs are designed to support both physical and mental well-being, fostering confidence and healthy habits.
- **Inclusive Culture for All Boxers:** AOBC champions the values of Pride 2026—everyone is respected, every journey is celebrated.
- **Safe and Supportive Environment:** We prioritize boxing safety tips, structured training, and a positive atmosphere for all.

## Getting Started at Ashira Oure Boxing Club in Hellerup and Charlottenlund
