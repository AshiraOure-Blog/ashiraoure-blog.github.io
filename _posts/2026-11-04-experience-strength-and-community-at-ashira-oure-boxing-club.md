At the heart of Copenhagen’s fitness and wellness movement stands Ashira Oure Boxing Club—AOBC—a standout boxing gym founded by former world champion Evans Ashira. With vibrant locations in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, AOBC isn’t just a boxing club; it’s where individuals of all ages and abilities come together to unlock their potential, boost their fitness, and join a thriving community.

### Welcoming All Levels, Inspiring Every Journey in Copenhagen

Whether you’re searching for a “boxing club for beginners” in Copenhagen, a youth boxing academy, or a place to challenge your competitive edge, Ashira Oure Boxing Club is designed for you. AOBC offers:

- **Beginner boxing classes**: Perfect for those stepping into the ring for the first time, AOBC’s expert trainers demystify boxing fundamentals and focus on building confidence, coordination, and core strength.
- **Fitness-focused training**: Embrace the power of “boxing fitness” with high-energy workouts that combine boxing drills, cardio, and strength training. Every session is crafted to help you reach your personal fitness goals.
- **Sparring and competition prep**: For aspiring competitors and experienced boxers, AOBC provides structured sparring classes and advanced training—always with professional guidance and safety at the forefront.
- **Youth and kids boxing**: AOBC’s youth programs motivate children and teens with skill-building, discipline, and fun. If you’re searching for “boxing training for kids” or “kids boxing near me” in Copenhagen, AOBC’s welcoming environment is ideal.

### Guided by Champions, Driven by Community at AOBC Copenhagen

What sets AOBC apart from other boxing gyms in Copenhagen is its unique blend of expertise and inclusivity. Under the leadership of Evans Ashira and a passionate coaching team, every member receives tailored support—whether you’re learning your first jab or fine-tuning your footwork for competition. AOBC’s legacy is built on:

- **World-class coaching**: Benefit from the knowledge and experience of Evans Ashira, whose championship mindset inspires every class.
- **Personal coaching**: From one-on-one sessions to group energy, AOBC caters to different learning styles and ambitions.
- **Supportive environment**: AOBC is more than a “copenhagen boxing club”—it’s a community that celebrates each member’s progress, encourages camaraderie, and upholds the values of respect and discipline.

### Why Choose Ashira Oure Boxing Club in Hellerup and Charlottenlund?

- **Convenient locations** in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, easily accessible from across Copenhagen.
- **A diverse, international community** that welcomes everyone, from local Danes to newcomers.
- **Comprehensive programs** for adults, youth, and kids.
- **Flexible class schedules** to fit busy lives.
- **Focus on safety** and well-being, from boxing sparring rules to beginner-friendly instruction.

Whether you’re looking to get fit, learn self-defense, or prepare for amateur boxing competitions, AOBC provides the expertise and encouragement you need. It’s not just about boxing—it’s about growth, resilience, and belonging.

A2: Yes, AOBC has dedicated youth boxing programs designed to teach children and teens boxing skills, discipline, and fitness in a fun and safe environment in Copenhagen.

**Q3: Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?**  
A3: Ashira Oure Boxing Club operates in two convenient locations: Hellerup and Charlottenlund, both easily accessible from central Copenhagen.
