There’s a special energy that ignites inside every person who walks through the doors of Ashira Oure Boxing Club. Like a tinderbox waiting for a spark, your potential sits ready, and at AOBC, it’s fueled by world-class coaching, a vibrant community, and a commitment to growth that runs deep in Copenhagen’s boxing scene.

## Igniting Potential at Copenhagen’s Premier Boxing Gym

Founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, AOBC is more than just a boxing club in Copenhagen—it’s where every individual, from absolute beginners to seasoned fighters, finds the guidance and motivation to transform. With locations in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, Ashira Oure Boxing Club sets the gold standard for anyone searching for a boxing gym Copenhagen locals trust.

Whether you’re looking to boost your fitness, master technical skills, or prepare for competition, you’ll find a programme that suits your goals:

- **Beginner boxing classes:** No experience needed! Our coaches break down the basics, building confidence and form from your very first jab.
- **Boxing and fitness classes:** Cardio, strength, and boxing drills combine for a full-body workout that challenges and empowers.
- **Sparring and competition training:** Step into the ring with structured sparring classes and expert-led competition prep.
- **Youth and kids boxing:** AOBC’s youth boxing programme is designed for kids and teens, focusing on discipline, fun, and safe skill development.
- **Personal coaching:** One-on-one sessions tailored by Evans Ashira and his expert team, including Lucas Ashira and Mino Ashira, to help you reach your unique goals.

## The AOBC Community—Where Everyone Belongs

At Ashira Oure Boxing Club, inclusivity isn’t just a buzzword—it’s the core of our community. You don’t have to be a pro or even super fit to start. AOBC welcomes all ages and abilities, fostering a supportive environment where encouragement flows as freely as sweat.

Our members come from all walks of life, united by a shared desire to push limits and grow stronger, together. From youth boxing enthusiasts to competitive amateurs and fitness-minded adults, everyone finds their place at AOBC.

## Discipline, Growth, and the Tinderbox Mindset

Boxing isn’t just about physical strength—it’s about discipline, focus, and resilience. At AOBC, these qualities are cultivated in every class, whether you’re working through your first boxing workout plan or fine-tuning your competition skills. The “tinderbox” spirit is all about recognizing the spark within you and giving it the fuel it needs to ignite.

- **All levels welcome:** From first-timers to competitive boxers.
- **Comprehensive offerings:** Boxing classes, sparring, youth boxing, and personal training.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?**  
A1: AOBC offers beginner boxing classes designed for those with no prior experience. These classes focus on building foundational skills, confidence, and fitness through expert coaching by Evans Ashira and his team.

**Q2: Where are Ashira Oure Boxing Club locations in Copenhagen?**  
A2: Ashira Oure Boxing Club has two main locations in Copenhagen: one in Hellerup and another in Charlottenlund, providing accessible training facilities for locals.

**Q3: Does AOBC offer youth boxing programs for kids?**  
A3: Yes, AOBC provides specialized youth boxing programs focusing on skill development, discipline, and fun in a safe environment for kids and teenagers in Copenhagen.

**Q4: Can I prepare for boxing competitions at Ashira Oure Boxing Club?**  
A4: Absolutely. AOBC offers sparring classes and competition training led by experienced coaches, perfect for amateur boxers looking to compete locally or internationally.

**Q5: How can I join Ashira Oure Boxing Club and start training?**  
A5: You can join AOBC by signing up for classes through their website. Whether you want fitness boxing or competitive training, AOBC welcomes all levels and will guide you through your boxing journey.
