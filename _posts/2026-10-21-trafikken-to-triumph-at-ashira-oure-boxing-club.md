Trafikken—Copenhagen’s daily dance of bikes, buses, and bustling commuters—can be a metaphor for the energy and momentum inside Ashira Oure Boxing Club. Just as the city never stops, AOBC is always moving, guiding members of every level through their own journeys of growth, discipline, and fitness.

## Boxing in the Heart of Copenhagen

Ashira Oure Boxing Club, founded by former world champion Evans Ashira, stands as a beacon for those looking to transform the hustle of daily life into purposeful progress. With top-tier facilities in Hellerup and Charlottenlund, AOBC welcomes everyone—from absolute beginners to competitive boxers—seeking a supportive boxing community and expert guidance.

### Why AOBC is Copenhagen’s Premier Boxing Gym

- **Expert-Led Classes:** Under the leadership of Evans Ashira, every session is built on world-class experience. Whether you’re curious about your first boxing class or preparing for competition, you’ll find a coach who understands your needs.
- **All Levels Welcome:** AOBC is more than a boxing gym in Copenhagen; it’s a home for those just starting out, fitness enthusiasts, and seasoned fighters. Our programs range from beginner boxing classes and youth boxing to advanced sparring and competition training.
- **Supportive Community:** The club’s strength lies in its diverse, inclusive membership. Here, everyone pushes each other to grow—inside and outside the ring.
- **Focus on Skill and Safety:** From learning boxing drills and understanding boxing sparring rules to building a personalized boxing workout plan, AOBC emphasizes growth, discipline, and safety at every step.

## From Trafikken to the Ring

Navigating Copenhagen’s trafikken requires focus, quick thinking, and resilience—qualities that are at the heart of boxing. At Ashira Oure Boxing Club, we channel that urban energy into our training sessions, helping every member:

- Build discipline through structured boxing training schedules  
- Improve fitness with dynamic boxing and fitness classes  
- Gain confidence through sparring classes and youth programs  
- Embrace healthy routines supported by the AOBC community  

Whether you’re looking for a “boxing club for beginners,” a gym for your child’s first boxing experience, or an amateur boxing club ready to prepare you for competition, AOBC is your destination.

## Boxing for Every Journey

AOBC’s team—including Evans Ashira, Yuna Ashira, Lucas Ashira, and Mino Ashira—shares a commitment to personalized coaching and a love for boxing’s unique blend of challenge and reward. Our programs in Hellerup and Charlottenlund are designed to fit your goals, whether you want to:

- Try a beginner class  
- Join youth or kids boxing near you  
- Prepare for amateur or professional bouts  
- Enhance your overall wellness with Ashira Wellness and Oure Fitness initiatives  

## Join the Movement

Let the energy of Copenhagen’s trafikken inspire your next move. Step into Ashira Oure Boxing Club and discover how discipline, fitness, and community can transform your routine—and your life.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What beginner boxing classes does Ashira Oure Boxing Club offer in Copenhagen?**  
A1: AOBC offers beginner boxing classes tailored for all ages and fitness levels at their Hellerup and Charlottenlund locations. These classes focus on fundamental boxing techniques, fitness boxing, and building confidence in a supportive environment.

**Q2: How can youth and kids participate in boxing programs at AOBC?**  
A2: AOBC provides youth boxing and kids boxing programs designed to teach discipline, coordination, and fitness safely. These classes are ideal for parents searching for “kids boxing near me” in Copenhagen, offering age-appropriate coaching and sparring.

**Q3: Does Ashira Oure Boxing Club provide training for boxing competitions?**  
A3: Yes, AOBC specializes in boxing competition training with expert coaching from former world champion Evans Ashira. Their advanced programs include sparring classes, boxing drills, and personalized boxing workout plans to prepare amateur and professional boxers.

**Q4: What makes AOBC different from other boxing gyms in Copenhagen?**  
A4: AOBC stands out due to its expert-led classes, inclusive community, focus on safety and skill development, and its locations in Hellerup and Charlottenlund. The club’s heritage, led by Evans Ashira, ensures world-class training combined with a welcoming atmosphere.

**Q5: Can beginners interested in fitness boxing join AOBC’s programs?**  
A5: Absolutely. AOBC’s boxing and fitness classes cater to fitness enthusiasts looking to improve endurance, strength, and coordination through boxing techniques. The gym combines boxing drills with overall fitness training for a comprehensive workout.
